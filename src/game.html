AWS AI-DLC v2 · interactive simulator · the office edition

Mission Control

Lead one intent through the AI-Driven Development Life Cycle v2: from idea to production and back again. Fourteen AI agents work the floor of a virtual office. Your team approves their work at every gate. Then the quarter-end surge hits production, and everything you approved decides how you fight it.

14 agents · 5 humans · 33 stages · one very bad quarter-end

Previously, in v1

The Identity Risk API team used AI-DLC v1 to ship ID-VEL-01: a warning when one identity appears on many loan applications at a single lender. v1 stopped at Build and Test. Operations was a placeholder.

Now: fraud rings spread one identity across many lenders, so each lender sees only one application. Lenders want a network-wide warning, ID-VEL-02, live in production before the quarter-end surge. It also has to stay up when the surge arrives.

Illustrative scenario: the product, people and figures are made up for training. Stage names, lead, support and reviewer agents, sensors and scope switches are taken from awslabs/aidlc-workflows release 2.11.4. Scoring, gate ownership and the incident are game rules.

How to play

01PredictGuess which agent leads each stage. Click its card, or the bot itself.
02DecideAnswer the questions the AI routes to your role. Your answers carry forward.
03GateRead the artifacts, weigh the sensors and the reviewer, then approve or request changes.
04SurviveTake on the production incident. What you approved earlier sets the tools you have.

Shortcuts: 1–4 pick an option · A approve · R request changes · Space skip waiting · M sound on/off. In the office: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, double-click to reset, hover anyone to see who they are.

1 · Choose your seat on the team

2 · Choose a scope (a scope, or workflow profile, is a stage list plus ceremony switches: sensors, reviewers, plan approval, collaborators)
