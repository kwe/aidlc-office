Unofficial simulator of AWS AI-DLC v2 · the desktop edition

Aegle API Team: Mission Control

Lead one intent through the AI-Driven Development Life Cycle v2: from idea to production and back again. Fourteen AI agents do the work and message you on the team's trusty old workstation. Your team approves their work at every gate. Then production pushes back, and everything you approved decides how you fight it.

▶

14 agents · 5 humans · 33 stages · 6 pieces of work

Previously, in v1

The Aegle API Team used AI-DLC v1 to ship ID-VEL-01: a warning when one identity appears on many loan applications at a single lender. v1 stopped at Build and Test. Operations was a placeholder.

Now the team runs AI-DLC v2, and the work keeps arriving: a network-wide warning that lenders want before quarter end, a brand-new product, a production bug, a security advisory, a refactor and a second region.

How to play

1. PredictGuess which agent leads each stage. Answer in the Conductor chat, or click the agent in the Buddies window.
2. DecideAnswer the questions the AI sends to your role. Your answers carry forward.
3. GateOpen the artifacts from the aidlc folder, weigh the sensors and the reviewer, then approve or request changes.
4. SurviveTake on the production incident. What you approved earlier sets the tools you have.

Keys: 1–4 pick a reply · A approve · R request changes · Enter continue after a teammate's stage · Space skip waiting · M sound on/off. Windows drag by their title bars and resize from the bottom-right corner.

Illustrative scenario: the product, people and figures are made up for training. Stage names, lead, support and reviewer agents, sensors and scope switches are taken from awslabs/aidlc-workflows release 2.11.4. Scoring, gate ownership and the incident are game rules. This is an unofficial training game, not affiliated with or endorsed by AWS. The desktop is a made-up operating system and is not affiliated with any real one. Source on GitHub.

Prefer a 3D office to an old desktop? Play the office edition.

Mission setup

Three choices, then launch.

1. Choose the work

Six different pieces of work land on the Aegle API Team. Each one suits different scopes.

2. Choose a scope for it

A scope, or workflow profile, is a stage list plus ceremony switches: sensors, reviewers, plan approval, collaborators.

Not sure? /aidlc compose has the Composer score five kinds of uncertainty in the work and propose a tailored plan.

3. Choose your seat on the team
